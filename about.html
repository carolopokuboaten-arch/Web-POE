Organisation History

Cream and Batter is a small bakery business launched by Ivy Shy,
it begun in 2023 and has been running for the past 3 years.
The small business offers different kinds of pastries ,
from your ordinary cookies to your lavish cakes .
The name behind cream and batter represents her grandmother's
famous cream cakes and a reminder that it all started in the kitchen,
it dwells on the passion she has on creating desserts that everyone can enjoy .
The business is more than just a bakery as it allows customers to indulge in authentic ingredients ,
cream and batter would like to expand the brand and reach an online audience while still serving her community.

Vision & Mission Statement

mission statement is to create desserts with exceptional and an empowering taste that will make her customers come back for more.
Her Vision is to always have the delight and pleasure of making any pastry with kindness and joy.
Cream and Batter's products are purposely targeted for workers looking for a quick bite, families hosting a gathering and parents planning birthday parties.
